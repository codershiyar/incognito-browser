Incognito Browser
Privacy policy

We collect nothing.

Last updated 26 August 2026

There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, and no server of ours for anything to be sent to. Nothing you do in this browser is recorded anywhere we can reach.

This page exists because Google Play requires one, and because an app that can ask for your camera, microphone and location owes you an explanation of exactly when and why.

What stays on your device

WhatWhat happens to it
Browsing historyNever written. There is no history feature to clear.
Cookies, cache, local storageDeleted when you close the app, and again when you open it
Your settingsKept on your device, so the app looks the way you left it
Downloaded filesKept in your Downloads folder. They are your files.

None of this leaves your device. There is nothing for you to request a copy of and nothing for us to delete, because we never had it.

What goes over the internet

Only the websites you visit. Your browser talks to those sites directly, exactly as any browser does. We are not in the middle of that and cannot see it.

Search terms go to the search engine you picked in Settings. What that company does with them is covered by their privacy policy, not this one.

Permissions, and when they are asked for

The app never uses any of these on its own. Each is requested at the moment a website asks for it, and only after you agree. Say no and the website is told no.

PermissionAsked for when
CameraA site asks for it, for example a video call or a QR scanner
MicrophoneA site asks for it
LocationA site asks where you are, for example a map
NotificationsNever requested. Declared only so you can switch the media playback controls on yourself, from Android settings.
InternetLoading web pages
See installed appsOpening links in the app they belong to, such as a banking or identity app. Without it Android hides those apps and every such link fails.
Foreground serviceKeeping audio playing when you switch away, for example a radio stream

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking libraries and no crash reporting. It ships with a list of known advertising and tracking domains, used to block requests to them. That list sits inside the app and is never fetched or updated over the network.

Google Play counts installations, as it does for every app in its store. That is Google's collection and is covered by Google's privacy policy, not this one.

Children

This app is not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If it ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. Since the app collects nothing, no change here can reach back and affect data that was never gathered in the first place.
