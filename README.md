# Incognito Browser

**Browse like you were never here.**

No history. No cookies. No cache. Everything from your session is gone the
second you close it, so whoever picks up your device next finds an empty
browser.

## Private is the only mode there is

Other browsers hide a private mode behind a menu you have to remember to
use. This one has no other mode. Every tab, every time, without you doing
anything.

**Nothing is written down.** Cookies, local storage, databases and the cache
are wiped, not hidden. There is no history feature to clear, because nothing
is ever recorded in the first place.

**Blank in the app switcher.** Open your recent apps and other browsers show
a live picture of the page you were reading. This one shows nothing.
You can still take a screenshot yourself whenever you want to. It is the
card other people see that is hidden, not your own use of the browser.

**Ads and trackers stopped.** Requests to ninety thousand known advertising
and tracking domains never leave your device. Pages come in lighter, load
faster and use less of your data. The list ships inside the app and is never
fetched over the network.

**Popups wait for you.** Sites that open windows on their own, or bounce you
somewhere you never asked to go, are held back and shown to you first. You
decide whether it opens.

**Cast to your TV.** Send video and audio from any page to a Chromecast, or
to a Samsung or LG over DLNA. Pages with several videos let you pick the one
you want.

**The same browser on your desktop.** A real Windows version, not a phone app
in a window. It registers with Windows as a browser you can set as your
default, opens files straight off your computer, and throws its entire
profile away when you close it.

### Windows will warn you the first time

It will say the publisher is unknown, because this app has no paid signing
certificate yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Every new program
without one gets this, and it goes away as more people install it.

If you would rather be certain the file is the one published here, check it
before running:

```powershell
Get-FileHash .\IncognitoBrowserSetup.exe -Algorithm SHA256
```

It should read:

```
377ab0cdf8065beb8385ea9401a3d3f913e25a802336945754013cae69b93d83
```

The installer needs no administrator rights. It installs for your user only,
adds a Start menu entry, and uninstalls cleanly from Windows Settings.

## What it does not do

No account. No sign-up. No sync, because there is no server to sync with.
No advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking libraries of
any kind.

Camera, microphone and location are only ever requested when a website asks
for them, and only after you allow it. Say no and the site is told no.

The full detail is in the [privacy policy](https://codershiyar.github.io/incognito-browser/privacy.html),
which is short, because there is not much to say when nothing is collected.
